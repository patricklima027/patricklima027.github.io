What we make

Paletas, flan, helados, fresas con crema and antojitos. New things show up on Instagram first.

Paletas

Sandía, pineapple with chocolate, Biscoff, mango with chile and more, on a stick.

Flan

Flan, tres leches flan, choco flan and Kinder Joy flan.

Helados

Helados de chicle, galleta María, elote, queso, Dubai chocolate and osito de gomitas.

Fresas con crema

Strawberries and cream with Biscoff, Nutella, queso or Dubai chocolate.

Antojitos

Fruit with chile, Mexican-style corn, crepes, concha loca, birria and more. Drinks and smoothies too.
