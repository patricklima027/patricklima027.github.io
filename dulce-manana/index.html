A little corner of home

Dulce Mañana is a sample bakery we designed to show what a site for a neighborhood spot can feel like: the window at night, the first tray of the day, the coffee window.

For a real client, this is where the owner's story goes: who opened the doors, where the recipes come from, and why the regulars keep coming back.
