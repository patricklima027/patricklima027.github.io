Questions people ask

Do I pay anything before I see my site?

No. I build a preview first. You pay $210 only after you approve the finished site. I never ask for passwords or payment up front.

What do you need from me?

Your Instagram or website, your hours, address, phone, menu or services with prices, and the links you want on the page (delivery, booking, social). I mark anything missing and ask you. I never make up details.

How long does it take?

Up to 72 hours after I have your info.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes. Two rounds of changes are included before launch. After that, the Care Plan ($25 a month) covers changes, or a single change is $25.

What about my domain and hosting?

Hosting is free on Cloudflare. A domain costs about $10 to $15 a year, bought in your name, and I walk you through it step by step. Once you've paid, I publish the site and send you the files and a short guide.

Can I take orders or bookings?

The site has your call, directions and order buttons, and links to the system you already use (Toast, Square, Clover, DoorDash, Calendly). A full online store with a cart is not part of the one-page site.

Are the sample sites real businesses?

No. They are made-up shops I designed to show what a site can feel like. Yours is built from your own photos, colors and words.

Where are you based?

In Brazil. I work remotely with businesses in the U.S., by message and email.

Will your name be on my site?

A small "Website by Noondoor" sits in the footer. If you'd rather not have it, ask and I remove it.

Do I need WhatsApp?

No. Pick what you already use: Instagram DM, email or WhatsApp.

Want to see yours first?
