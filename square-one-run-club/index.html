Questions

Do I need to be fast?

No. The leader waits at every corner and the last runner in gets the loudest cheer.

I've never run a 5K.

Come at 6:45, say it's your first time and take the walk-run group. It leaves first, so you won't be last.

Does it cost anything?

No. The pints do.

What if it rains?

We run in rain. With lightning we move to a covered patio and post the call by 4 PM.

Do I have to sign up?

You can just show up. Signing up gives you a bib number and helps us count pace groups.

What should I bring?

Shoes, water and something reflective or a small light. The squares are dark after sunset.

Cross the line.

Pick a pace, type your first name, get your bib. Walk-ups are fine too: the bib only saves us counting.
