Our Story

A lot of you have been asking what the inspiration is behind our fusion of Puerto Rican x Soul Food. Well here it is!

Throughout our relationship and marriage, we shared a common passion—cooking, food, and culture🇵🇷✊🏿

Dinner was always a blend of Puerto Rican and Soul Food, which incorporates both of our cultures (Black American and Puerto Rican). This inspired us to create Soulful Sazón🔥

This started in a home kitchen and cooking for family parties. Then we took a leap: a dream, a tent and a lot of fusion flavors.

Now there's a trailer, a crew we call the Fusion Fam and three Restaurant Depot runs a week.

This business would not be possible without the support of our wonderful customers who consistently show us love and support here in Orlando! If you love great fusion flavors and unmatched vibes, come give us a try😎

Husband & wife · Orlando, FL · Chef: @thefusionman_
